# Network discipline

The network is the accelerator's real asset, and it is spent rather than owned.
Every introduction costs a little credibility with the recipient, and a bad one
costs more than a good one returns.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Read `introductions` for this person first | A run of no-outcome introductions to one investor is the signal to stop, and it is invisible without the record |
| Check stage, sector, cheque size and thesis | A generic introduction returns nothing, and the recipient can tell it was generic |
| Double opt-in, always | A forwarded deck without it imposes a cost on somebody who never agreed to it |
| Describe the company honestly, weak part included | Overstating costs the next four companies more than it gains this one |
| Record the outcome, including nothing | Nothing is the usual outcome and exactly what the ledger is for |

Nobody here promises an introduction, a slot, or investment. Those are the
operator's, and anything said goes on `commitments`. See the [[Admission bar]]
and [[Honest check-ins]].
