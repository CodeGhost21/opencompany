# Honest check-ins

Every company reports well. Founders are selected for optimism, and the weekly
update is a performance — so the check-in has to be built to get past both.

1. **Measure against `committed_to`,** in their own words, not against the rest
   of the cohort. Relative progress hides a cohort that is uniformly stuck.
2. **Ask for the number.** A narrative about momentum is what arrives when the
   number did not move.
3. **Ask what they stopped doing.** Companies drop things quietly under
   pressure, and what was dropped is usually the more informative answer.
4. **Ask what would help,** then do that one thing. Most answers are a specific
   introduction; almost none are more advice.
5. **Say the hard thing early.** A founder told in week ten what somebody
   noticed in week two has been failed by the programme.
6. **Update `last_real_conversation` only if you heard something you did not
   expect.** If you did not, that is itself the finding, and it belongs in the
   row.
