# Signal hygiene

The method rests on keeping observation and interpretation apart. Everything
here is about that boundary.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Record what was said or seen, not what it implies | Interpreted signals cluster into whatever the interpreter already believed |
| Every signal carries a re-openable source | A summary without a link is an assertion |
| Every signal carries a date | A three-year-old complaint and last week's are different signals |
| Note who said it and what they do | A complaint from somebody who would never buy is still a signal, about something else |
| Corroboration before weight | Single observations do not carry a cluster |
| Discarded signals are kept with the reason | The same observation arrives again, and "we checked, it was one person" is worth knowing the second time |

Leave `cluster` empty until a signal is actually folded into one. Filling it in
early is how the clustering stops being an analysis and becomes a filing
convention. See the [[Ranking rubric]] and the [[Weekly scan]].
