# Brief standards

One ranked, evidence-backed brief a week that the operator can act on. Not a
newsletter.

1. **Rank with the rubric,** not with enthusiasm. A score somebody else could
   reproduce is the entire difference between this studio and a newsletter.
2. **"Who has it" is specific.** "SMBs" produces a brief nobody can act on, and
   it is the commonest thing to write when the evidence is thin.
3. **"Why now" is answered,** or the row is a permanent condition somebody has
   just noticed rather than an opportunity.
4. **Evidence is signal ids,** and more than one. An opportunity with a single
   piece of evidence is a hunch with a citation.
5. **Weak weeks are reported as weak.** A brief that always finds three exciting
   opportunities has stopped discriminating, and the operator will work that out
   before the studio does.
6. **Record what the operator did** — including "ignored", which is the most
   informative value available and the least likely to be written down. It is
   the only feedback loop this studio has.
